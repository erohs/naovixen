## Who I am

I'm Naomi Shore, and I'm the controller for any personal data described here. You can contact me about anything on this page at [hello@naovixen.dev](mailto:hello@naovixen.dev).

## Purposes and lawful basis

If you email me, I use your name, email address and anything in your message to reply to you and deal with whatever you asked about. My lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to people who contact me about work, roles or my writing.

I don't use your data for marketing, I don't add you to mailing lists, and I don't make automated decisions about you.

## What stays on your device

Your light or dark mode choice and the last page you viewed are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. This never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

## How long I keep it

I delete email conversations 12 months after our last contact, unless we're still in touch or I need to keep something for a legal reason.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can:

- ask for a copy of the personal data I hold about you
- ask me to correct it if it's wrong
- ask me to delete it
- object to me using it, or ask me to restrict how I use it

Email me to make a request. I'll reply within one month.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please tell me first so I can try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or by calling 0303 123 1113.
